TV Home privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

What the app gets from Google

When a profile signs in with Google, TV Home asks only for the basic profile: the account's name, email address and profile photo (the openid, email and profile scopes). It does not ask for access to Gmail, Drive, YouTube, contacts or any other Google data.

How it is used and stored

The name and photo are shown on that profile in "Who's watching?" and on the home screen. They, the email address and a Google refresh token are stored only on the TV, in the app's private storage, so the name and photo can be refreshed from Google every minute while the home screen is showing.

Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server other than Google's. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no data is sold or shared.

Weather

For the weather, the city set on the TV is sent to Open-Meteo to look up the forecast. No account information is sent with it.

Removing your data

Delete the profile on the TV, or uninstall the app, to remove everything it stored. You can also remove TV Home's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
